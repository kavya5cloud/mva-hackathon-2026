# Final evidence table — Track 2

**Frozen 2026-09-24.** Every row states what the evidence *is*, not what we would
like it to mean.

**Two rules applied throughout:**
1. **A supporting observation is not pathogenic evidence.** Rarity, conservation
   and *in-silico* pathogenicity scores are listed as supporting observations for
   `p.Asn1002Lys`; none is counted as evidence that it is pathogenic.
2. **Correlated computational predictions are not independent experimental
   evidence.** AlphaMissense, SpliceAI/Pangolin, DynaMut2 and the AlphaFold-based
   structural work are all Tier 3. They are trained on overlapping features
   (conservation, sequence context, predicted structure) and their agreement is
   **not** replication. Zero experimental assays have been performed in this project.
